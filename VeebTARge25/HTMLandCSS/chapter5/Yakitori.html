Yakitori (grilled chicken)

A traditional Japanese street food

Recipe:
500g chicken breast
2 tbsp soy sauce
1 tbsp honey
1 tbsp mirin
1 garlic clove, minced
Bamboo skewers

Cut the chicken into small pieces and place them on skewers. Mix soy sauce, honey, mirin, and garlic in a bowl. Brush the sauce over the chicken and grill for 10–12 minutes, turning often until golden brown.
