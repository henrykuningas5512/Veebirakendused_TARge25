Okonomiyaki (savory pancakes)

A famous Japanese savory pancake

Recipe:
2 cups flour
1 cup water
2 eggs
2 cups shredded cabbage
100g sliced meat or seafood
1 tbsp soy sauce
Okonomiyaki sauce

Mix flour, water, and eggs into a batter. Add cabbage and soy sauce, then pour into a frying pan. Add meat or seafood on top and cook both sides until golden brown. Finish with Okonomiyaki sauce.
