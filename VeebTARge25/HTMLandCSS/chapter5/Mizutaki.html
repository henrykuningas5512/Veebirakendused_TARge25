Mizutaki (chicken stew)

A warm traditional Japanese hot pot

Recipe:
500g chicken pieces
1 liter chicken broth
1 carrot, sliced
1 onion, chopped
1 cup mushrooms
1 cup cabbage
2 tbsp soy sauce
Salt and pepper

Add chicken broth to a pot and bring to boil. Add chicken and vegetables, then simmer for 25–30 minutes. Season with soy sauce, salt, and pepper before serving hot.
