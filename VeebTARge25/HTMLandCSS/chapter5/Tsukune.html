Tsukune (minced meat patties)

A delicious Japanese chicken meatball dish

Recipe:
500g minced chicken
1 egg
2 tbsp breadcrumbs
1 tbsp soy sauce
1 tsp grated ginger
1 garlic clove, minced
Salt and pepper

Mix all ingredients in a bowl and shape into small patties or meatballs. Grill or fry for 8–10 minutes until golden brown. Serve with sweet soy sauce or rice.
